About us

Your device. Your rules. Your software.

We're a collective defending a simple principle: when you buy a device, it's yours. Really yours. Not half-yours, not conditionally yours, not yours only as long as you stay locked inside the manufacturer's ecosystem.

That basic idea has somehow become radical. Locked bootloaders, spare parts crippled by "parts pairing," updates that strip away features after a year of use, prices that shift depending on your phone or your location, sensors quietly reporting data you never agreed to share. Dispossession has been normalized, that's why we've decided to push back.

We work on three fronts: we advocate to change laws and industry practice, we inform people about what's actually happening, and we build free tools so everyone has a way out, starting now, without waiting for the law to catch up.

The right to own your devices

A device you can't repair, modify, or understand isn't really yours, it's a lease disguised as a purchase. We advocate for:

  • Unlocked bootloaders on every device, with no unreasonable trade-off or loss of warranty
  • Control over your operating system, including pressure on manufacturers to open-source or document their drivers
  • The right to repair: fair-priced spare parts, accessible diagrams and diagnostic tools, and an end to software locks that invalidate legitimate replacement parts
  • An end to planned obsolescence, with a guaranteed minimum period of security support
  • The right to resell or give away a device without it being crippled once it changes hands
  • Interoperability, the freedom to switch providers or repair shops without losing access to your own features or data

The right to privacy

Surveillance isn't the exception anymore, it's become the default business model. We believe it's your call what you share, not the platform's. We advocate for:

  • Protection against tracking, whether ad-based, fingerprinting, consent-driven or extracted through deceptive interfaces
  • An end to personalized pricing based on your device, location, or browsing history
  • Protection against mass surveillance, public or privatized, deployed without real democratic debate
  • End-to-end encryption by default, with no backdoors
  • Real data portability, the ability to export your data and leave without artificial friction

Our tools

We don't just ask nicely. While the law catches up with technical reality, we build free, open tools so people can take back control right now. Our first project is a browser extension in development, aimed at neutralizing dynamic pricing based on your device's data. All our projects are free and open-source, with auditable code and zero data collection on our end.

Campaigns & watchdogging

We publicly document what large companies would rather keep quiet: GDPR non-compliance, privatized surveillance, and attacks on device ownership. Our goal isn't to react after the fact, it's to make visible what's being put in place before it becomes a structural problem.

Why now

None of this is set in stone. Unlocked bootloaders have existed before. The right to repair is already advancing in some countries. GDPR, flaws and all, shifted the balance of power. But none of it is guaranteed either, it has to be defended, actively, continuously. That's what we're here to do.

Association

Atoile is a non-profit association under the French law of 1901 dedicated to supporting the right to repair and digital ownership of devices, while developing and distributing free and open-source software.
