# Privacy Policy

This page explains what data is processed when you visit atoile.org, and how.

## What we don't do

- We do not use any cookies.
- We do not carry out any tracking or audience analytics.
- We do not display any advertising.
- We do not share any data with third parties.
- We do not create any user accounts.

## Server technical logs

The site runs on an nginx web server, using its default logging configuration. This default configuration automatically records, for each visit:

- the visitor's IP address,
- the date and time of the request,
- the page or resource requested,
- the HTTP response code,
- the user-agent (browser/system used),
- the referring page, if any.

These logs are generated automatically by the server software for technical purposes (operation, security, incident diagnosis) and are not used for any other purpose. They are not sold, not shared with third parties, and not cross-referenced with other data.

## Local storage

The site offers, on an **optional basis and only when you actively enable it** (opt-in), the ability to save your language preference in your browser's local storage (localStorage).

- This information stays only on your device.
- It is never sent to our servers.
- You can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

## Legal basis and retention period

The processing of technical logs is based on the publisher's legitimate interest in ensuring the security and proper functioning of the site (Article 6.1.f GDPR). No specific retention period has been defined to date; the association is working to document, and where appropriate reduce, this duration.

## Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and French data protection law, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and restrict the processing of your data. You can exercise these rights by writing to: contact@atoile.org.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL): www.cnil.fr.
