I’m **TBVNS**. I’m an Epita student, a heavy Arch Linux user, and the founder of **ATOILE**.

My origin story *isn't* some grand political awakening. It started simply because I wanted to root my phones. Then I found out Samsung was locking down bootloaders, and the AppCloud scandal broke. That was the breaking point. I realized it wasn't just about losing root access anymore; it was about seeing exactly how much control manufacturers exert over the hardware we actually pay for. We were being treated like tenants in our own digital homes, and I got tired of it.

That’s why I started **ATOILE**.

On the technical side, I’m a software developer, but my job here is specific: I build the tools. While the advocacy side of ATOILE pushes for long-term legal changes, I write the code that gives people a way out right now. I manage the dev projects, build the extensions, and make sure our software does exactly what it’s supposed to do—staying completely free, auditable, and collecting zero data.

I want to live in a world where we actually own what we buy. Until the law and the industry catch up, I’ll keep writing the code to make sure we can.
